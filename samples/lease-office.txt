COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE

This Lease is made on 1 June 2026 between Corran Property Holdings plc ("Landlord") and Fennwick Studio Ltd ("Tenant") for Suite 4B, 22 Marlow Street, Manchester (the "Premises").

1. Grant. Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant for the Term, subject to the conditions below.

2. Term. The initial term is twelve (12) months starting 1 July 2026 (the "Initial Term"). On expiry of the Initial Term, and on expiry of each subsequent term, this Lease shall automatically renew for a further term of twenty-four (24) months on the same conditions unless Tenant gives Landlord written notice of non-renewal not less than one hundred and eighty (180) days before the then-current expiry date. Notice given at any other time is void. Rent for each renewal term increases by eight percent (8%) over the preceding term.

3. Rent. Tenant shall pay GBP 3,200 per month in advance on the first day of each month by standing order. Late payment attracts interest at 4% above the Bank of England base rate from the due date.

4. Deposit. Tenant shall pay a deposit equal to three months' rent on signing, to be held by Landlord and returned within 30 days of the end of the Lease less any sums properly due.

5. Use. The Premises may be used only as offices for Tenant's design business. Tenant shall comply with all applicable laws and the building rules in Schedule 1.

6. Repair. Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair, fair wear and tear excepted. Landlord shall maintain the structure, roof, common parts and building services.

7. Tenant's Liability and Indemnity. Tenant shall be liable for, and shall indemnify Landlord against, any and all loss, damage, claims, fines and expenses arising directly or indirectly from Tenant's occupation of the Premises or the building, including loss of rent from other tenants, loss of profit and consequential loss, regardless of cause and whether or not caused by the negligence of Landlord, its agents or contractors. There is no cap on Tenant's liability under this Lease.

8. Insurance. Landlord shall insure the building. Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than GBP 2,000,000 and contents insurance.

9. Termination. Landlord may terminate this Lease at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice, with or without cause, and on such termination Tenant shall vacate without compensation. Tenant has no right to terminate before the end of the then-current term.

10. Alterations. Tenant shall not make structural alterations without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for non-structural fit-out.

11. Assignment. Tenant shall not assign or sublet without Landlord's prior written consent.

12. Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
